## 2. Information stored on your device

The information you enter can include wallet names and balances; transaction types, dates, amounts, fees, currencies, payees and notes; budgets and categories; income sources; goals and contributions; recurring items; debt records; and manual investment records such as a holding name, provider or location, broad asset category, cost basis, current value, valuation date and note. Free-text names and notes may contain personal information if you choose to put it there.

If you choose to paste or share a supported mobile-money confirmation, Mizani reads its text on this device. The original message is discarded after review; only the transaction you confirm and its provider reference are stored in your ledger.

Mizani also stores settings such as your selected country, primary currency, theme and app-lock preferences, optional reminder settings and time, and cached currency rates. App-lock details stay on this device. The app records which versions of these documents you accepted and when.

## 3. Why the app uses this information

Mizani uses your information to provide the budgeting features you choose, protect your records, and create exports or backups when you request them. Optional daily and commitment reminders check your local ledger and use generic notifications without names or amounts; they do not send ledger information off the device.

Mizani does not require a name, email address, phone number, bank login, or account. Your ledger is not used for advertising or usage tracking.

If you choose **Send feedback**, Mizani opens the email app on your device with your message addressed to the **Mizani team**. Mizani does not attach ledger records or send the message itself. Your email provider receives anything you choose to send and controls delivery and spam filtering.

## 4. Optional requests

When you convert a foreign-currency transaction, Mizani sends the two currency codes to Frankfurter to get the latest available reference rate. It does not send the amount, date, payee, note, wallet name, or other ledger details. The rate and date are saved with the transaction on your phone.

Frankfurter says it neither collects nor logs personal data, IP addresses, or request details. Your network provider still handles the connection. Read more at <https://frankfurter.dev/>.

If you installed Mizani outside Google Play, you can choose to check for updates. Mizani requests public release information from GitHub and compares the release version on your device. It does not send your installed version or ledger. GitHub may receive your IP address and request details under its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). You can skip the check.

When you use Send feedback, the email app you choose may process and retain the message under its own terms. The feedback message includes the text you write and basic Mizani and Android version information; it does not include transaction amounts, balances, wallet names, or backup data unless you put them in the message yourself.

## 5. Storage, security and retention

Your financial records are encrypted and stored on this phone. Device security helps protect access to them. No security measure can guarantee that a device or data is safe in every circumstance.

Mizani sets Android's secure-window protection on app screens, dialogs and menus to block ordinary screenshots, screen recordings, and non-secure display capture. Android and device behavior varies; this cannot prevent capture with an external camera or on a compromised device.

Mizani keeps no online copy of your ledger. Local records remain in the app until you delete all local data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Delete all data also clears saved currency rates, settings and reminder schedules. Automatic device backups are disabled. Uninstalling the app or losing access to this phone may make local data unrecoverable.

## 6. Exports and other services

CSV exports and Excel workbooks are unencrypted and may expose your financial details to anyone who can access the file. The CSV is a transaction-ledger export; the Excel workbook and encrypted backup also include manual investment records. The workbook includes multiple sheets such as transactions, wallets, investments, budgets, recurring schedules, goals, debts and commitments. Imported transactions may include the provider and reference needed to prevent importing the same confirmation twice. A Mizani backup is encrypted with the passphrase you choose. When you save or restore a file, Android’s file picker and any destination service you select may process or store it under their own terms and privacy notice. Mizani cannot delete those external copies for you.

## 7. Other services

The provider does not receive or sell an online copy of your ledger, or use it for advertising. External services receive only the information described above or a file you choose to save. The operating system, app marketplace, and network providers may process technical information under their own terms. Feedback sent through your email provider is not a copy of the Mizani ledger; avoid including financial details in it.

## 8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can view and correct entries in the app, export your ledger from Settings, and delete local data from Settings. Deletion does not remove exports or backups already saved elsewhere. Currency-rate requests happen only when you convert a foreign-currency transaction. Update checks are optional and available when you install outside Google Play. Neither sends ledger entries. Optional reminders use local settings and check local records only. Shared or pasted transaction text is processed on this device and is not retained as a message.

Privacy rights depend on the law that applies where you live. That law may give you rights to request access, correction or deletion, object to certain processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. You can manage records stored only on this device in the app. For a request about information the provider handles, email paul.ochieng.dev@gmail.com. Do not email ledger details unless needed to handle your request.

## 9. Children

Mizani is not designed to collect information from children. Anyone below the age of legal capacity should use it only with permission from a parent or legal guardian.

## 10. Changes

If this Notice changes materially, the app will show it and ask you to accept the new documents before you continue. You can read the Terms and Notice from Settings at any time. Contact paul.ochieng.dev@gmail.com with a privacy question.
